## Decisions and limits

- The guard cannot stop: a direct write to a file the checkout rewrites; a late writer that forks the
  operation log; power loss; raw `git` in a colocated repository.
- The adoption rollback in `manage.py` still uses `jj op restore`, guarded by an operation-id check. Removing
  it would leave a half-adopted project. Not changed.
- Read helpers do not use `--ignore-working-copy`. A guard rejection does no jj read, so the operation log
  stays unchanged.
- The operation-fork writer class and `op-heads-moved` are covered by pyjutsu's suite, not CopyRoom's.
- The nix-meta branch moves repoman from v0.7.5 to v0.10.0 and takes 44 Vendomat commits (V5 source
  collection). Run `nixos-rebuild` only after you review it.
- The prebuilt pyjutsu wheel needs Linux x86-64 with glibc 2.39 or later.
- The earlier `step-2.5/2026-10-08-race-and-guard/SUMMARY.md` says no lane was published. That is out of date.
